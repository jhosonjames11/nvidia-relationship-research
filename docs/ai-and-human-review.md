# AI and human review record

## AI assistance and required human validation

AI may assist with candidate discovery, code drafting, and wording review. AI output is not evidence verification and does not determine a relationship conclusion.

Humans must validate entities, source access, quotations, relationship classification, score inputs, and final conclusions before a confirmed relationship is committed. Human review also checks relationship direction, scope, timing, source metadata, and that wording does not imply unsupported exclusivity, volume, continuation, causality, or investment advice.
